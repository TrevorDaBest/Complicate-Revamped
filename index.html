Welcome to Complicate Revamped, the new version for complicate. I made he priginal as a fun project and I like it. But now, I made Complicate Revambed, the new version. With 2 distict gamemodes and many tiers, you are bound to have a great time.

This Game is Meant for People Tacking 8th Grade Math or Beyond.

The Rules (Complicate Game Mode)

It's relatively simple. You get an expression/equation and you and your friends take turns to complicate it. However, if you believe a complication is wrong, you can press "Vote" for everyone to evaluate it. If the majority says your complication is right, they lose points. If the majority says you're wrong, you lose points. If you have more complex symbols within the tier, the more points you get. You have 30-60 seconds to complicate the equation.

Tiers

  1. Tier 1: Pre-Algebra/Algebra 1 - You can only use arithmetic symbols, roots, exponents, ±, π. You lose points if you use more complex symbols.
  2. Tier 2: Algebra 2/Pre-Calculus - You can use things in Tier 1 as well as: trigonometric functions, logrithms, e, i, ∞, exp() function, limits, matricies. You lose points if you use more complex symbols.
  3. Tier 3: Calculus - You can use things in Tier 1 and 2 as long as: derivatives (and partial derivatives), integrals (and multivariable integrals), summations, hyperbolic trigonometric functions, vector operations, etc. You gain more points if you use more complex symbols.
  4. Tier 4: God - You can use any math symbol you wish. You gain more points if you use more complex symbols.

The Rules (Guess the Function Game Mode)

It's also relatively simple. You and your team gets a graph of f(x). Everyone gets 5 tokens at first and you can use them to find: f(a) (1 token), solve for x when f(x) = a (2 tokens), or graph f'(x) or f(x) + g(x) (3 tokens). You can chat with your team members to take ideas for f(x). If you guess it, your teammates win. You get 3 tries.

Tiers

  1. Tier 1: Pre-Algebra/Algebra 1 - Linear, Quadratic, Exponential, Radical functions.
  2. Tier 2: Algebra 2 - Polynomial, Reciprocal, Trigonometric, Logrithmatic, Simple Composite functions.
  3. Tier 3: Pre-Calculus - Complex Composite, Polar Functions.
  4. Tier 4: God - Differential Equation Functions, Special Integral Functions, etc.
